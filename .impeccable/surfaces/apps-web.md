# Surface brief: apps/web (whole site)

Scope: every route of the static Next.js site. Mode: Operate (citizens look things up; Read on /glosario and /metodologia).
Audience and job: a neighbour on a cheap phone, often outdoors, finds their municipality, sees what it spends on and why, and what to ask. Content is real MEF data; no invented claims.
Constraints: static export, GitHub Pages (<1 GB), Spanish, 375 px first, WCAG AA, light on data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The budget announced like a cartel chicha: the question and the number a citizen needs, set in stacked fluorescent bands you can read from across the street. Refuses the category default of a white civic dashboard of same-size cards with a blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black poster ground (#0E0E10) with fluorescent ink fields: fucsia #FF2E88 (identity, attention), amarillo #FFE600 (amounts, primary action), verde #00D26A (on pace, increases), naranja #FF8A00 (behind, cuts), celeste #3EE0FF (explain/links). Tall condensed block display (Anton) for bands and headings, Archivo for reading and tabular figures. Printed marks only: flat ink, 1px rules, no soft shadows, no rounded cards.

STORY: The visitor sees in one glance what the State (or their municipality) has and has spent, understands each category through "¿Qué es?", and reaches their district in two taps; alerts read as reasons to ask, not accusations.

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone 390x844, home): black top bar with the wordmark and the cut date ("al 17 oct"); a stack of three bands, fucsia "¿En qué se gasta", amarillo "el dinero del Perú?", verde "S/ 289.8 mil millones · 2026"; two yellow-ruled figures (gastado, avance vs esperado); the municipality search directly below as the primary action; bottom tab bar. Entity pages use the same stack with the entity's name.

FORM: Cartel chicha (my pick, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list), raised with: tabular numerals on one right-aligned axis (Datamatics); every section headed by its own band tab (high-density portal); states as printed marks, 1px rules (centre-rail edition); the route país › región › provincia › distrito always visible (transit diagram); the grid as armature (Crouwel). Signature move: the band stack, revealed once per page with a left-to-right ink wipe (clip-path, staggered, exponential ease-out; off under reduced motion), and budget splits drawn as colour bands. seed:15c2ab040177a04f
